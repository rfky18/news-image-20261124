## Heroes (sub-headline)

1. **Nvidia soars as Huang returns** — WORLD · TECH · New York.
   Shares closed up 31% on Monday 23 Nov after the board reinstated
   Jensen Huang as chief executive, president and director; the rally
   spread across Asian markets on Tuesday (Nikkei up about 5%, Kospi
   7.4%). Sources: Reuters, CNN, Investopedia.
2. **Russian jets trigger Korea alert** — ASIA · SECURITY · Ulleungdo.
   Several Russian military aircraft entered the Korea Air Defence
   Identification Zone over the East Sea on Tuesday morning (~07:52
   KST); a shelter warning for Ulleung County was lifted after about
   five minutes. The Joint Chiefs of Staff said there was no violation
   of South Korean airspace; fighter jets were scrambled, and the
   Defence Ministry planned to summon Russia's defence attaché.
   Sources: Yonhap, Korea JoongAng Daily, AJP.
3. **Trump unveils Japan trade deal** — ASIA · TRADE · Tokyo.
   Washington announced a trade deal with Japan plus terms with
   Indonesia; Tokyo stocks led an Asian rally while the EU readied
   tariffs on $84bn of US goods. Sources: Reuters, Access24.

## Cards 4–13

4. **After COP31 collapse, focus turns to reform** — WORLD · CLIMATE.
   COP31 ended on Sunday 22 Nov without a pact; on 24 Nov the UN
   climate chief Simon Stiell said he would seek advice on reforming
   a process in which one country can block a deal. COP32 meets in
   Ethiopia in November 2027. Sources: BBC, UN News.
5. **Refinery fire burns on after drone strike** — EUROPE · WAR.
   The Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery in Rostov region was still ablaze
   on 24 Nov after Ukrainian long-range drones struck it overnight;
   the plant halted operations. Sources: Kyiv Independent, Liveuamap.
6. **Libya east seeks UN probe over Ukraine claims** — AFRICA · DIPLOMACY.
   Authorities in eastern Libya asked the UN to investigate reports
   of Ukrainian troops in the west, after Ukrainian sea drones struck
   Russian ships at Benghazi. Sources: C54, Reuters.
7. **O'Hare ground stop lifted as holiday rush builds** — AMERICAS · TRANSPORT.
   The FAA briefly halted arrivals at Chicago O'Hare over air-traffic
   staffing on Monday 23 Nov; flights resumed ahead of the
   Thanksgiving travel peak. Sources: FAA, AP.
8. **Lawmakers protest Huang's return to Nvidia** — AMERICAS · POLITICS.
   Members of Congress objected to the board reinstating Jensen Huang
   as Nvidia shares posted their best day since 2003. Source: Reuters.
9. **India holds third place in Asia Power Index** — ASIA · DIPLOMACY.
   India retained its ranking in the Lowy Institute index.
   Sources: Careerindia, Lowy Institute.
10. **Rescue goes on at factory fire site** — ASIA · DISASTER · Cikande.
    Emergency teams kept searching for missing workers after a major
    fire at an industrial facility; the cause is under investigation.
    Sources: Careerindia, Antara.
11. **Himalayan flood: 4 dead, 100 missing** — ASIA · DISASTER.
    The army searched for scores unaccounted for after a wall of muddy
    water tore down a narrow mountain valley. Sources: Access24, AP.
12. **RSF drones hit Sudan power stations** — AFRICA · WAR.
    Drone attacks on power stations triggered mass blackouts across
    Sudan. Sources: C54, Reuters.
13. **South Africans vote as ANC faces discontent** — AFRICA · POLITICS.
    Voters went to the polls with service-delivery failures testing the
    governing party; police barred anti-migrant rallies outside a
    court. Sources: C54, Reuters.
