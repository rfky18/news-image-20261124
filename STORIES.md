## Heroes (sub-headline)

1. **Nvidia soars as Huang returns** — WORLD · TECH · New York.
   On his first day back on Tuesday, Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia
   headquarters as shares held Monday's 31% gain and the rally spread
   across Asian markets (Nikkei up about 5%, Kospi 7.4%). The board
   reinstated Huang as chief executive, president and director on
   Monday 23 Nov. Sources: Reuters, CNN, Investopedia.
2. **Russian jets trigger Korea alert** — ASIA · SECURITY · Ulleungdo.
   Several Russian military aircraft entered the Korea Air Defence
   Identification Zone over the East Sea on Tuesday morning (~07:52
   KST); a shelter warning for Ulleung County (about 9,000 residents)
   lasted about five minutes. The Joint Chiefs of Staff said there was
   no violation of South Korean airspace; fighter jets were scrambled,
   and the Defence Ministry planned to summon Russia's defence attaché.
   Sources: Yonhap, Korea JoongAng Daily, Reuters.
3. **Trump unveils Japan trade deal** — ASIA · TRADE · Tokyo.
   Washington announced a trade deal with Japan plus terms with
   Indonesia; Tokyo stocks led an Asian rally while the EU readied
   tariffs on $84bn of US goods. Sources: Reuters, Access24.

## Cards 4–13

4. **After COP31, focus turns to reform** — WORLD · CLIMATE.
   COP31 ended on Sunday 22 Nov without a pact; on 24 Nov the UN
   climate chief Simon Stiell said he would seek advice on reforming
   a process in which one country can block a deal. COP32 meets in
   Ethiopia in November 2027. Sources: BBC, UN News.
5. **Refinery fire burns after drone strike** — EUROPE · WAR.
   The Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery in Rostov region was still ablaze
   on 24 Nov after Ukrainian long-range drones struck it overnight;
   the plant halted operations. Sources: Kyiv Independent, Liveuamap.
6. **Israeli strike kills three in Gaza** — MIDDLE EAST · WAR.
   An Israeli strike killed at least three Palestinians on Tuesday
   near a police outpost in Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip,
   local health officials said; five more people were wounded.
   Source: Reuters.
7. **O'Hare stop lifted for holiday rush** — AMERICAS ·
   TRANSPORT. The FAA briefly halted arrivals at Chicago O'Hare over
   air-traffic staffing on Monday 23 Nov; the ground stop was lifted
   on Tuesday 24 Nov and flights resumed ahead of the Thanksgiving
   travel peak. Sources: FAA, AP.
8. **Lawmakers protest Huang's return** — AMERICAS · POLITICS.

9. **EU leaders set to agree ECB team this week** — EUROPE · ECONOMY.
